I presented research at the **IFE Conference** (Institute for the Future of Education) alongside **Dr. Victor Rodriguez**.

## Research Focus

Our collaboration explored the future of AI in education, examining:

- **AI-powered personalized learning** systems
- **Intelligent tutoring** technologies
- **Assessment automation** and feedback
- **Educational data mining** and analytics
- **Ethical frameworks** for AI in academia

## Collaboration

It was a great pleasure to work with Dr. Victor Rodriguez on this research. The IFE Conference brings together educators, researchers, and technologists to discuss innovations that will shape the future of learning.

The intersection of artificial intelligence and education presents tremendous opportunities to improve learning outcomes while also raising important questions about equity, privacy, and the role of human educators.
