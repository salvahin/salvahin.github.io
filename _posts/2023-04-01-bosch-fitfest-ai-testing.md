I participated as a panelist at **Bosch FitFest 2023**, where we discussed the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence applied to software testing.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/news/2023-04-bosch-fitfest.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" caption="Panel discussion at Bosch FitFest 2023" %}

The panel brought together industry experts to explore how AI is revolutionizing quality assurance processes:

- **Automated test generation** using machine learning
- **Visual testing** with computer vision
- **Intelligent test prioritization** and selection
- **Predictive analytics** for defect detection

## Panelists

I was joined by excellent colleagues:
- Sergio Jair Martín Alvarez
- Jose Luis Anaya Bernal
- Armando Ramirez

The discussion highlighted both the opportunities and challenges of integrating AI into existing testing workflows, with practical insights from real-world implementations at Bosch and other organizations.
