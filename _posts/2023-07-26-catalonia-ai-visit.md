As part of the **fAIrLAC Jalisco** program, a delegation from Tecnológico de Monterrey's AI Hub visited Catalonia to explore their thriving artificial intelligence ecosystem. The week-long mission provided invaluable insights into how a region can strategically develop AI capabilities.

## Catalonia.IA Strategy

We had the opportunity to learn about **Catalonia.IA**, the regional government's comprehensive AI strategy. The approach integrates:

- Research excellence at universities
- Hospital and healthcare partnerships
- Industry collaboration frameworks
- Ethical AI guidelines

## Key Visits

### Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC)
Met with researchers Raul Benitez Iglesias and Christian Mata, who focus on health and AI applications. Their work on diabetic retinopathy detection and cardiotoxicity assessment showcased the potential of AI in clinical settings.

### Healthcare Innovation
- **Hospital de la Santa Creu i Sant Pau**
- **Barcelona Children's Hospital**
- **Xartec Salut** health technology cluster

### Research Centers
- **CIDAI** - Catalonia's AI research center
- **CataloniaBio** - Life sciences cluster

## Topics Explored

Our discussions covered critical themes for responsible AI development:

- **Healthtech applications** - From diagnosis to treatment optimization
- **Ethics in AI** - Building frameworks for the LAC region
- **Women in AI** - Initiatives to increase female participation
- **AI democratization** - Making AI accessible to non-technical audiences

## Building Bridges

Special thanks to:
- The **Catalonia Government Delegation in Mexico and Central America** for facilitating this knowledge exchange
