I participated as a panelist at **MICAI 2025** in a discussion titled **"Generative AI's Impact on Education"**.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/news/2025-10-micai-panel.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" caption="Panel on Generative AI in Education at MICAI 2025" %}

## Panel Discussion

The panel explored critical questions about how generative AI is reshaping educational practices:

- **Academic integrity** in the age of AI assistants
- **Personalized learning** through AI tutors
- **Assessment redesign** for AI-enabled environments
- **Preparing students** for an AI-augmented workforce
- **Ethical considerations** in AI education tools

## Distinguished Panelists

I was honored to share the stage with:
- **Omar Florez**
- **Pablo Rivas**
- **Inés Alvarez-Icaza**
- **Xiaoou Li**
- **Lourdes Martinez**

The panel was moderated by **Gilberto Ochoa-Ruiz**.

The diverse perspectives from academia and industry made for a rich discussion on both the opportunities and challenges that generative AI presents for educators and students alike.
