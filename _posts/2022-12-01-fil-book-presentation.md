I had the honor of presenting our collaborative book at the **Feria Internacional del Libro de Guadalajara (FIL)**, one of the most prestigious book fairs in the Spanish-speaking world.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/news/2022-12-fil-book.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" caption="Book presentation at FIL Guadalajara 2022" %}

The FIL Guadalajara brings together authors, publishers, and readers from across Latin America and beyond. It was a privilege to share this work with fellow co-authors and connect with attendees passionate about advancing knowledge in our field.

Special thanks to all the co-authors who made this publication possible, and to everyone who attended the presentation.
