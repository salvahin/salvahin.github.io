I'm honored to serve as Guest Editor for the Special Issue **"Innovations in Artificial Intelligence for Medicine and Healthcare"** in Health Information Science and Systems (Springer).

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/news/2025-01-special-issue.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" caption="Special Issue announcement" %}

## About the Special Issue

This special issue explores cutting-edge applications of AI in healthcare, including:

- **Medical image analysis** and computer-aided diagnosis
- **Natural language processing** for clinical documentation
- **Predictive models** for patient outcomes
- **AI-assisted drug discovery** and development
- **Wearable devices** and remote patient monitoring

## Editorial Team

I'm working alongside an excellent group of co-editors:
- **Sharib Ali**
- **Christian Mata**
- **Lucía Prieto Santamaría**
- **Enrique Garcia Ceja**
- **Estefanía Talavera**

We're excited to showcase innovative research that bridges the gap between AI technology and clinical practice, ultimately improving patient care and healthcare delivery.
